NEXORA TIME WEBSITE
====================

Included:
- Home, Shop, About, Contact pages
- Responsive black + gold luxury theme with blue accents from the logo
- 10 uploaded product images
- WhatsApp ordering buttons
- Product category filters
- Mobile navigation
- Contact form that opens WhatsApp

IMPORTANT:
Open script.js and replace:
  const WA_NUMBER = "8801XXXXXXXXX";
with the store's WhatsApp number, country code included and without + or spaces.

PRODUCT DATA:
Product names/categories are editable directly in the HTML files.
Prices are intentionally shown as "Price on request" because no final prices were supplied for these uploaded product photos.

To preview:
Open index.html in a browser.
